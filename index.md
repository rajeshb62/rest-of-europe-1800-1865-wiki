# Rest of Europe 1800–1865 Wiki

A narrative-research knowledge base on continental European history from the close of the Napoleonic Wars through the mid-nineteenth century — the Restoration era, the Revolutions of 1830 and 1848, the national movements, and the transformations of the European state system. The United Kingdom is treated separately in its own wiki.

## Sources

### Treitschke — *Deutsche Geschichte im neunzehnten Jahrhundert*

- **Vol. I: Bis zum zweiten Pariser Frieden** (1879). The opening volume of Treitschke's Prussian-nationalist history, covering the long decline of the Holy Roman Empire from the Peace of Westphalia through the Wars of Liberation and the Congress of Vienna. [Read source page](sources/treitschke-deutsche-geschichte-vol1.md)

- **Vol. II: Bis zu den Karlsbader Beschlüssen** (1882). The early years of the German Confederation, the constitutional struggles of the South German states, the Burschenschaft movement, and the repressive Carlsbad Decrees of 1819. [Read source page](sources/treitschke-deutsche-geschichte-vol2.md)

- **Vol. III: Bis zur Juli-Revolution** (1885). The decade 1819–1830 from the Carlsbad Decrees to the July Revolution — the Congresses of Troppau, Laibach, and Verona, the formation of the Zollverein, the Greek War of Independence, the first stirrings of the Schleswig-Holstein question. [Read source page](sources/treitschke-deutsche-geschichte-vol3.md)

- **Vol. IV: 1830–1840** (7th ed., 1919). *Das Eindringen des französischen Liberalismus* — the shock of the July Revolution, constitutional movements, the Hambacher Fest, the Göttingen Seven, the Cologne Church conflict. [Read source page](sources/treitschke-deutsche-geschichte-vol4.md)

- **Vol. V: 1840–1848** (4th ed., 1899). The reign of Friedrich Wilhelm IV of Prussia — the Rhine crisis, Deutschkatholizismus, the Zollverein's expansion, social unrest, the United Diet of 1847, the gathering storm of the March Revolution. [Read source page](sources/treitschke-deutsche-geschichte-vol5.md)

### Sybel — *Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I.*

- **Vol. I: 1806–1850** (4. rev. Aufl., 1892). From the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire through the 1848 Revolution, the Frankfurt National Assembly, and the Erfurt Union. [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol1.md)

- **Vol. II: 1850–1856** (2. unv. Aufl., 1889). The Olmütz Punctuation, the Dresden Conferences, Germany during the Crimean War, the early reign of Wilhelm I, the entry of Bismarck. [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol2.md)

- **Vol. III: 1846–1864** (3. Aufl., 1890). The origin of the German-Danish War — the Schleswig-Holstein question from Christian VIII's open letter through the London Protocol, the Federal Execution, Danewerk and Düppel, the peace of 1864. [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol3.md)

- **Vol. IV: 1865–1866** (3. unv. Aufl., 1890). The break between Austria and Prussia, the Gastein Convention, the Prussian-Italian alliance. (This volume straddles the 1865 boundary; Vols. V–VII covering 1866–1870 live in the [1866–1919 wiki](../Rest%20of%20Europe%201866-1919/wiki/index.md).) [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol4.md)

### Political Economy

- **Friedrich List** — *Das nationale System der Politischen Ökonomie* (1841). The foundational text of the German historical school of economics, arguing for economic nationalism and the protective tariff philosophy that shaped the Zollverein. [Read source page](sources/list-nationales-system.md)
