# Treitschke — Deutsche Geschichte im neunzehnten Jahrhundert, Vol. II: Bis zu den Karlsbader Beschlüssen

**Heinrich von Treitschke (1834–1896)** — *Deutsche Geschichte im neunzehnten Jahrhundert. Zweiter Theil: Bis zu den Karlsbader Beschlüssen.* — Leipzig: Verlag von S. Hirzel, 1882.

The second volume of Treitschke's monumental five-volume history, published three years after the first. It is itself Volume 25 of the series *Staatengeschichte der neuesten Zeit*.

The volume covers the early years of the German Confederation, from the Congress of Vienna's settlement through to the Carlsbad Decrees of 1819. Treitschke's preface notes that the period required far more original archival research than the first volume, and apologises for the greater density of the subject-matter — "Ungelehrte Leser werden leider einiger Selbstüberwindung bedürfen um sich in den spröden Stoff zu finden" (non-academic readers will unfortunately need some self-discipline to find their way into this austere material). He acknowledges that the confused, party-myth-encrusted history of the period can only be mastered through detailed exposition, and explains he has had to spread the events up to 1830 across two volumes.

The narrative focus remains on all-German and Prussian affairs, with the smaller states treated where they become significant for the fate of the whole fatherland. The volume draws extensively on unpublished archival materials from Berlin and Vienna.

**Contents (Zweites Buch — continued from Volume I):**

### Dritter Abschnitt — Geistige Strömungen der ersten Friedensjahre (p. 3)
*The intellectual and cultural currents of the early peace years*

- **Literarischer Charakter des Zeitalters** — the literary character of the age; the mood of the nation after the Wars of Liberation; the retreat from political engagement into private and aesthetic life
- **Dichtung und bildende Künste** — poetry and the visual arts; Romanticism in its late phase; the beginnings of Biedermeier culture
- **Die Wissenschaft** — the state of the sciences; the rise of the German university system; the philosophical schools

### 4. Die Eröffnung des Deutschen Bundestages (p. 118)
*The opening of the German Federal Diet*

- **Europäische Lage** — the European situation after the Congress of Vienna; the great-power alignments
- **Die Frankfurter Verhandlungen** — the negotiations in Frankfurt; the establishment of the Bundestag; its character, procedures, and early impotence

### 5. Die Wiederherstellung des preußischen Staates (p. 181)
*The restoration of the Prussian state*

- **Personen und Parteien am Hof** — personalities and parties at the Prussian court; the conflict between reformers and reactionaries
- **Die Reorganisation der Verwaltung** — the reorganisation of the Prussian administration; the bureaucratic apparatus
- **Die Provinzen** — the Prussian provinces; the newly acquired Rhineland and Westphalia; the problem of integrating diverse territories
- **Der Beginn des Verfassungsstreites** — the beginning of the constitutional struggle; the promise of a constitution and its deferral

### 6. Süddeutsche Verfassungskämpfe (p. 295)
*The constitutional struggles of the South German states*

- **Das gute alte Recht in Schwaben** — "the good old right" in Swabia; the Württemberg constitutional conflict; the stand against monarchical centralisation
- **Bayern** — Bavaria's early constitutional experiment under King Maximilian I Joseph and Montgelas; the 1818 constitution
- **Baden** — Baden's 1818 constitution; the liberal experiment under Grand Duke Karl
- **Nassau und Darmstadt** — the smaller states of Nassau and Hesse-Darmstadt; their constitutional development

### 7. Die Burschenschaft (p. 383)
*The student fraternity movement*

- **Jahn und die Turner** — Friedrich Ludwig Jahn and the gymnastics movement; the nationalist Turnerschaften
- **Thüringen. Weimar und Jena** — Thuringia; the role of Weimar and the University of Jena as centres of student nationalism
- **Das Wartburgfest** — the Wartburg Festival of 1817; the student gathering that symbolised liberal and national aspirations; the burning of reactionary books

### 8. Der Aachener Congreß (p. 444)
*The Congress of Aachen (Aix-la-Chapelle), 1818*

- **Wachsende Macht des österreichischen Hofs** — the growing power of the Austrian court under Metternich
- **Räumung Frankreichs. Erneuerung des Vierbundes** — the evacuation of France; the renewal of the Quadruple Alliance
- **Deutsche Angelegenheiten auf dem Congresse** — German affairs at the Congress; the great powers' attitude toward German constitutional questions

### 9. Die Karlsbader Beschlüsse (p. 491)
*The Carlsbad Decrees, 1819*

- **Schwankungen in Berlin. Erste constitutionelle Erfahrungen im Süden** — hesitation in Berlin; first constitutional experiences in the southern states
- **Kotzebues Ermordung. Die Demagogenverfolgung** — the murder of August von Kotzebue by the student Karl Ludwig Sand; the ensuing demagogue-hunts
- **Teplitz und Karlsbad** — the conferences at Teplitz and Carlsbad; Metternich's orchestration of the repressive Decrees; press censorship, university supervision, and the dissolution of the Burschenschaft

### 10. Der Umschwung am preußischen Hofe (p. 573)
*The reversal at the Prussian court*

- **Die Karlsbader Beschlüsse und das Ausland** — the Carlsbad Decrees and their reception abroad; the European dimension
- **Der Verfassungsplan Hardenbergs. Humboldts Entlassung** — Hardenberg's constitutional plan; Wilhelm von Humboldt's dismissal; the defeat of the reform party
- **Der erste preußische Zollvertrag** — the first Prussian customs treaty; the beginnings of the Zollverein

### Beilagen zu den ersten zwei Bänden (Appendices for Volumes 1 and 2)

- **I. E. M. Arndt und Bücher** — correspondence and documents concerning Ernst Moritz Arndt and the publisher
- **II. Blücher über die Lütticher Meuterei** — Blücher on the Liège mutiny of 1815
- **III. Die Teplitzer Punktation** — the Teplitz agreement between Prussia and Austria
- **IV. Hardenbergs Verfassungsplan** — Hardenberg's draft constitution for Prussia
- **V. Hardenberg über die Ministerkrisis vom Jahre 1819** — Hardenberg on the ministerial crisis of 1819

The volume closes with a **Berichtigungen** (errata) page.
