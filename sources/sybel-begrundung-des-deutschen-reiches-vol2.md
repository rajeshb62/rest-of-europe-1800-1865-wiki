# Sybel — Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I., Vol. 2

**Heinrich von Sybel (1817–1895)** — *Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I. Zweiter Band.* — 2. unveränderte Auflage. München und Leipzig: Verlag von R. Oldenbourg, 1889.

The second volume of Sybel's seven-volume history, covering the period from the aftermath of the Olmütz Punctuation (1850) through the early years of Bismarck's ministry (1862). This volume traces the restoration of the German Confederation under Austrian leadership, Germany's stance during the Crimean War, the beginning of the New Era under Wilhelm I's regency, and the entry of Otto von Bismarck into Prussian politics at the height of the constitutional conflict.

Approximately 500 pages, from a Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (BSB) scan.

## Chapters

### Fünftes Buch: Herstellung des Bundestags (Restoration of the Bundestag)

*The restoration of the German Confederation's Federal Diet after the collapse of the Prussian Union.*

- **1. Kapitel: Graf Brandenburg in Warschau** — Count Friedrich Wilhelm von Brandenburg's mission to Tsar Nicholas I of Russia; negotiations with Prince Felix zu Schwarzenberg over the future of Germany
- **2. Kapitel: Olmützer Punktation** — The Olmütz Punctuation (November 29, 1850); Prussia's surrender to Austrian and Russian demands; the end of the Prussian Union project
- **3. Kapitel: Die Dresdener Konferenzen** — The Dresden Conferences (December 1850 – May 1851); the attempt to reform the German Confederation; the failure of the reform proposals

### Sechstes Buch: Deutschland zur Zeit des Krimkriegs (Germany during the Crimean War)

*Germany's position and internal developments during the Crimean War (1853–1856).*

- *(Chapters covering the Crimean War period, including:)*
  - The conflicting positions of Austria and Prussia during the Eastern crisis
  - Austrian mobilization and the threat of war with Russia
  - Prussia's policy of armed neutrality under Friedrich Wilhelm IV
  - The Zollverein vs. the Steuerverein (customs union competition between Prussia and Austria)
  - The German fleet question and the sale of the German Federal Fleet (Bundesflotte)
  - The impact of the Peace of Paris (1856) on German affairs

### Siebentes Buch: Erste Regierungsjahre Wilhelm's I. (Early Reign of Wilhelm I)

*The beginning of the New Era in Prussia.*

- *(Chapters covering the Prussian regency beginning 1858:)*
  - The appointment of Prince Wilhelm as Regent (October 1858) after Friedrich Wilhelm IV's incapacitation
  - The dismissal of the reactionary Manteuffel ministry
  - The New Era (Neue Ära) liberal ministry
  - Prussian foreign policy and the search for a new German policy
  - The rise of the German National Association (Nationalverein)
  - The Italian War of 1859 and its effect on German national sentiment

### Achtes Buch: Eintritt des Ministeriums Bismarck (Entry of Bismarck's Ministry, 1862)

*The Prussian constitutional conflict and Bismarck's appointment.*

- *(Chapters covering the Prussian constitutional conflict (Heeresreformkonflikt):*
  - King Wilhelm I's military reform plans (the Heeresreform)
  - The liberal majority in the Prussian Landtag
  - The conflict over military appropriations
  - Wilhelm I's abdication crisis
  - The appointment of Otto von Bismarck as Minister President (September 1862)
  - Bismarck's "Blood and Iron" speech and the beginning of the constitutional conflict era
