Treitschke — Deutsche Geschichte im neunzehnten Jahrhundert, Vol. III: Bis zur Juli-Revolution

Heinrich von Treitschke (1834–1896) — Deutsche Geschichte im neunzehnten Jahrhundert. Dritter Theil: Bis zur Juli-Revolution. — Leipzig: Verlag von S. Hirzel, 1885. (Volume 29 of the series Staatengeschichte der neuesten Zeit.)

The third volume of Treitschke’s five-volume history, covering the decade 1819–1830 — the period from the Carlsbad Decrees to the eve of the July Revolution. Treitschke’s preface notes the enormous archival burden of this volume, drawing on the Berlin Geheime Staatsarchiv, the papers of the Baden Bundestag envoy Freiherr von Blittersdorff, the correspondence of the Nassau statesmen Marschall and Röntgen, and the memoirs of the Darmstadt minister du Thil, among others. The volume traces the consolidation of Metternich’s system, Prussia’s internal struggles after Hardenberg, the emergence of the Zollverein, and the first stirrings of the national movement that would erupt in 1830.
