# Sybel — Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I., Vol. 3

**Heinrich von Sybel (1817–1895)** — *Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I. Dritter Band.* — 3. Auflage. München und Leipzig: Verlag von R. Oldenbourg, 1890.

The third volume of Sybel's seven-volume history, devoted entirely to the Schleswig-Holstein question and the German-Danish War of 1864. This volume traces the origins of the conflict from the constitutional history of the duchies through the succession crisis of 1846, the London Protocol of 1852, and the federal execution against Denmark in 1863–64, culminating in the military campaign across the Danewerk, Düppel, and Alsen, and the Peace of Vienna.

The volume covers the period 1846–1864 and is central to understanding how the Schleswig-Holstein crisis became the catalyst for Prussian-led German unification.

Approximately 500 pages, from a Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (BSB) scan.

## Chapters

### Neuntes Buch: Ursprung des deutsch-dänischen Kriegs (Origin of the German-Danish War)

*The historical and legal origins of the conflict over Schleswig-Holstein.*

- **1. Kapitel: Die alte Verfassung Schleswig-Holsteins** — The ancient constitution of Schleswig-Holstein; the traditional rights and privileges of the duchies; the Union of the Estates (Stände); the relationship between Schleswig and Holstein
- **2. Kapitel: Die Thronfolgefrage** — The succession question; King Christian VIII's open letter (1846) concerning the Danish succession and the status of the duchies; the reaction in Germany and Denmark
- **3. Kapitel: Die Verträge von 1852** — The treaties of 1852; the London Protocol (May 8, 1852); the international settlement of the succession question; the recognition of Christian of Glücksburg (later Christian IX) as heir
- **4. Kapitel: Der Londoner Vertrag** — The London Treaty; the great power guarantee of the Danish monarchy's integrity; the unresolved tensions over the duchies' separate status

### Zehntes Buch: Die Bundesexecution (Federal Execution against Denmark, 1863–64)

*The German Confederation's federal execution (Bundesexekution) against Denmark.*

- *(Chapters covering the federal execution, including:)*
  - The death of King Frederick VII of Denmark (November 1863)
  - Christian IX's accession and the November Constitution (November 1863)
  - The German Confederation's declaration of federal execution against Denmark for violating the London Protocol
  - The occupation of Holstein by Saxon and Hanoverian troops (December 1863)
  - The diplomatic maneuvering between Austria, Prussia, and the other German states
  - The alliance between Prussia and Austria (January 1864)
  - The ultimatum to Denmark and the beginning of hostilities

### Elftes Buch: Danewerk und Düppel (Dannewerk and Düppel)

*The military campaign of 1864.*

- *(Chapters covering the campaign, including:)*
  - The defensive lines of the Danewerk (Dannevirke); the Danish fortifications
  - The Prussian advance under Prince Friedrich Karl
  - The Danish evacuation of the Danewerk position (February 1864)
  - The retreat to Düppel (Dybbøl)
  - The siege of the Düppel entrenchments
  - The storming of the Düppel redoubts (April 18, 1864) — the decisive battle of the war
  - The role of Prussian artillery and infantry tactics

### Zwölftes Buch: Alsen — Friedensschluss (Alsen — Peace Settlement)

*The final operations and the peace.*

- *(Chapters covering the end of the war, including:)*
  - The capture of the island of Alsen (Als) by Prussian forces (June 29, 1864)
  - The final Danish collapse
  - The armistice negotiations
  - The Peace of Vienna (October 30, 1864)
  - The cession of Schleswig, Holstein, and Lauenburg to Prussia and Austria
  - The condominium arrangement for the administration of the duchies
  - The significance of the war for German unification

## Notes

The 3rd edition of 1890 is a revised reprint. The Schleswig-Holstein volume is particularly valuable as Sybel had access to Prussian and Austrian state archives for the diplomatic history. The raw text is extracted from a BSB scan (OCR from Google Books).
