## Scope

This volume covers the decade 1830–1840, described as "Das Eindringen des französischen Liberalismus" (The Penetration of French Liberalism). The Vorwort is dated Berlin, 30 November 1889.

The central theme is Germany's reaction to the July Revolution in France, the spread of constitutionalism, the consolidation of the Zollverein, and the growing pre-eminence of Prussia in German affairs.

## Key Themes

1. **The July Revolution (1830)** and its shockwaves through Germany — the Belgian crisis, the Polish uprising, the European concert
2. **The rise of constitutionalism** in the German states — Brunswick, Hesse, Saxony, Hanover, the Hambacher Fest (1832)
3. **Metternich's counter-offensive** — the Six Articles, the Frankfurt Wachensturm, the Munichgrätz agreement, renewed Vienna ministerial conferences
4. **The Zollverein's consolidation** — the decisive customs unification of Germany (minus Austria) under Prussian leadership
5. **"Young Germany"** — the literary and philosophical movement after Goethe, the Junghegelianer, Strauss's *Life of Jesus*
6. **The Hanoverian crisis (1837)** — the Göttingen Seven, the end of the 1833 constitution
7. **The Cologne Church conflict** — the Prussian state vs. the Catholic church under Droste-Vischering
8. **Prussia's rise** — Treitschke's thesis that by 1840 Prussia had moved decisively to the centre of German national life
