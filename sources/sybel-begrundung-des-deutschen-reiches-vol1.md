# Sybel — Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I., Vol. 1

**Heinrich von Sybel (1817–1895)** — *Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I. Erster Band.* — 4. revidirte Auflage. München und Leipzig: Verlag von R. Oldenbourg, 1892.

The first volume of Heinrich von Sybel's monumental seven-volume history of the founding of the German Empire under Wilhelm I. Sybel was a leading figure of the Prussian school of historiography, a pupil of Ranke, and a liberal nationalist who later became director of the Prussian State Archives. This work draws extensively on Prussian archival sources and presents a detailed narrative of German unification from the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire through the failure of the 1848 revolutions and the Prussian Union project.

The volume covers the period 1806–1850, organized into four books (Bücher). It begins with retrospective chapters tracing the historical roots of the Austro-Prussian dualism, the Napoleonic era, the early Bundestag, and the Vormärz period. The core of the volume treats the Revolution of 1848: the March revolutions, the Frankfurt Parliament, the failure of the unity project, and the subsequent Prussian Union initiative culminating in the crisis of 1850.

Approximately 500 pages, from a Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (BSB) scan.

## Chapters

### Erstes Buch: Rückblicke (Retrospectives)

*Retrospectives from the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire through the Vormärz period.*

- **I. Älteste Zeit** — The earliest period; the historical foundations of the German nation
- **II. Österreich und Preußen** — The dualism of Austria and Prussia; the development of the two great German powers
- **III. Fremdherrschaft und Befreiungskriege** — Foreign domination (the Napoleonic era) and the Wars of Liberation (1813–1815)
- **IV. Erste Jahre des Bundestags** — The early years of the German Confederation's Federal Diet (Bundestag), 1815–1830
- **V. Einwirkung der Julirevolution** — The impact of the French July Revolution of 1830 on Germany
- **VI. Friedrich Wilhelm IV.** — The character and policies of King Frederick William IV of Prussia (reigned 1840–1861)

### Zweites Buch: Erster Versuch der deutschen Einheit (First Attempt at German Unity, 1848)

*The revolution of 1848 and the first attempt to unify Germany through the Frankfurt Parliament.*

- **1. Kapitel: Die Märzrevolution** — The March Revolution; the Heidelberg Assembly; Metternich's fall; the Berlin March days of 1848
- **2. Kapitel: Die Parteien** — The political parties; the Vorparlament (Pre-Parliament); the Baden rebellion; Friedrich Christoph Dahlmann's constitutional draft
- **3. Kapitel: Nationalversammlung und Reichsverweser** — The Frankfurt National Assembly; Heinrich von Gagern; the election of Archduke Johann of Austria as Imperial Regent (Reichsverweser)
- **4. Kapitel: Verwicklungen** — Complications; Ludolf Camphausen's ministry; the Grundrechte (Fundamental Rights); the Poland debate; republican stirrings and radical agitation

### Drittes Buch: Scheitern des Einheitswerkes (Failure of the Unity Project)

*The collapse of the Frankfurt Parliament's attempt to create a unified German state.*

- **1. Kapitel: Schleswig-Holstein** — The Schleswig-Holstein question; the Malmö armistice (August 1848); the reaction in the National Assembly
- **2. Kapitel: Die Nationalversammlung und die Märzerrungenschaften** — The National Assembly and the achievements of the March revolution; the growing counter-revolutionary movement
- **3. Kapitel: Die Reichsverfassung** — The Imperial Constitution (Paulskirchenverfassung) of March 1849; the offer of the imperial crown to Frederick William IV; his refusal

### Viertes Buch: Die preußische Union (The Prussian Union)

*Prussia's attempt to achieve German unification on its own terms after the failure of Frankfurt.*

- **1. Kapitel: Das Dreikönigsbündnis** — The Three Kings' Alliance (Dreikönigsbündnis) of May 1849 between Prussia, Saxony, and Hanover
- **2. Kapitel: Der Unionsentwurf** — The draft constitution of the Prussian Union (Erfurter Union)
- **3. Kapitel: Die Unionspolitik** — The politics of the Union; the Erfurt Parliament (March–April 1850); the growing opposition from Austria and the middle states
- **4. Kapitel: Die Crisis** — The crisis of 1850; the threat of war between Prussia and Austria; the Humiliation of Olmütz
